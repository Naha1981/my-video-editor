Privacy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

NahaVideo processes footage, audio, images, website URLs and project information that you submit to the service. The service uses that information to perform media analysis, editing, rendering and delivery operations you request.

Media

Uploaded media may be stored on the deployment's configured storage so projects can be resumed. Operators should not upload material they are not authorized to process. Configure retention and storage according to your deployment and client agreements.

External services

Optional integrations such as NahaLLM, Jev and self-hosted Cobalt are disabled unless configured by the operator. Requests to those services may include the information needed to perform the requested operation.

Website intake

When you provide a public website URL, NahaVideo retrieves publicly accessible page content to compile a creative brief. Private or local network destinations are blocked.

Logs

Operational logs may record request paths, timing, status and a request identifier. Avoid placing sensitive client information into free-text fields.

Your responsibility

NahaVideo is a production tool. The operator remains responsible for lawful use, media rights, licensing, retention periods and client consent.
